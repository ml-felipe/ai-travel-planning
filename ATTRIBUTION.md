# Provenance and redistribution

Recovered from Felipe Martínez's UPC-FIB Artificial Intelligence Drive folder:

- P2/`reglas-v5.clp` → `expert-system/rules.clp`.
- P2/`ontologia-v8.clp` → `expert-system/ontology.clp`.
- P3/pddl/`dominio-viajes.pddl` and its `problemas/` directory → `planning/`.

The ontology header explicitly credits owl2clips for translating the ontology into CLIPS. Its generated classes and accessors must not be presented as handwritten code. The original header, including its source ontology filename and generation date, is preserved. The ontology and bundled demonstration instances are retained because the working expert system requires them.

The rule pipeline and planning model are recovered coursework implementations. A matching starter baseline, ontology editing history and collaborator records were not available, so exclusive individual authorship is not claimed. The repository owner confirmed on 24 November 2026 that they hold the necessary redistribution rights for the coursework and included third-party material. Original author credits and copyright notices are retained; no new license is assigned to inherited code.

Migration additions are the README, dependency pin for tests, and the two verification scripts. Recovered CLIPS and PDDL contents are unchanged. Reports, duplicate versions, backups and historical execution traces are omitted.
